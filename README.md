# SMART-ECOSPHERE
Smart EcoSphere is an interactive, single-page web application designed to visually engage users in environmental awareness and sustainable living. By blending immersive web graphics with responsive design, the platform serves as an interactive hub for exploring eco-friendly concepts, planetary data, and green initiatives.
Smart EcoSphere is an intuitive, futuristic single-page web dashboard designed to empower users with real-time analytics on household resource consumption, waste reduction, and overall climate footprint. By integrating food expiration alerts, pantry inventory tracking, energy usage estimation, and carbon offset metrics into a seamless interface, Smart EcoSphere makes sustainable living accessible and quantifiable.

✨ Key Features🥬 1. Food Expiration TrackerWaste Prevention: Tracks items approaching their expiration date (e.g., within 3 days) to prompt timely consumption.Overdue Alerts: Automatically flags products that have already passed their shelf life to avoid accidental consumption and assist in waste auditing.
🧺 2. Pantry Restock ManagerAutomated Restock Indicators: Displays a real-time count of low-stock or depleted items needing replenishment.Quick List Access: Links directly to an interactive restock list to streamline grocery planning and curb over-purchasing.⚡ 3. Energy Consumption & Cost MonitorUsage Estimation: Calculates and displays estimated monthly power consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh).Financial Metrics: Converts energy usage into live local currency estimates (e.g., ₹8,640) based on configured utility rate tariffs.
🌍 4. Climate Impact & $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ Offset CalculatorTotal Emissions Avoided: Aggregates carbon savings measured in kilograms of $\text{CO}_2$ equivalent ($\text{CO}_2\text{e}$).Multi-Source Breakdown: Computes contributions across connected subsystems:FreshVault: Food preservation & waste diversion savings.Pantry Optimization: Sustainable sourcing & reduced duplicate purchasing.Electricity Optimization: Energy efficiency adjustments.
